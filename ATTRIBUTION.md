# Patch 19 research attribution

This patch contains original simulation code. Biological mappings were implemented from
published descriptions and MaleCNS annotation identities; no article text/data table is
redistributed.

Key references:
- Tastekin et al., Cell (2026), DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.016 — adult Drosophila
  gustatory connectome and receptor/type assignments.
- Muench & Galizia, Scientific Reports (2016), DOI 10.1038/srep21841 — DoOR 2.0
  olfactory response consensus and receptor/glomerulus/ligand relationships.
- Manzo et al. (2012), Motor neurons controlling fluid ingestion in Drosophila —
  MN11/MN12 pump/ingestion roles.
- Drosophila mechanosensation/Johnston's-organ literature summarized in insect
  mechanosensation reviews and Virtual Fly Brain/FlyBase annotations.
- Virtual Fly Brain/FlyBase MaleCNS terms for HRN_VP4, HRN_VP5, TRN_VP1m,
  HRN_VP1l, TRN_VP2 and TRN_VP3 sensory identities.
